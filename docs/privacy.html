Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Aura Assistant ("Aura") is a desktop app that runs on your own Windows PC. The developer does not run any server for Aura and does not collect, receive, store or sell any of your data. Everything Aura saves stays in a folder on your PC (%LOCALAPPDATA%\Aura), and you can delete it at any time.

Google user data

Connecting Google is optional. If you do, Aura asks for these permissions and nothing else in your account:

PermissionWhat Aura does with it
Google Tasks
auth/tasks		 Adds a task when you ask for a reminder or a to-do (for example "add buy milk to my Google Tasks").
Google Calendar events
auth/calendar.events		 Adds an event with an alert when you ask for a timed reminder, so it rings on your phone. Reads your upcoming events to show them in Aura's notch (calendar and "Up next").
  • Your Google data goes directly between your PC and Google. It is never sent to the developer or to any other company.
  • Aura does not share, sell or transfer Google user data, and does not use it for advertising.
  • Google data is not used to train AI models. Event titles are not sent to any AI service.
  • The sign-in token is saved only on your PC (google_token.json in Aura's folder).
  • You can disconnect at any time by deleting that file, or by removing Aura at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Aura's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other data Aura handles

To do what you ask, Aura sends only what that task needs, directly from your PC to the service you set up:

  • Your requests go to the AI provider you choose in Settings (Groq, Google Gemini or OpenRouter with your own API key), or to an AI command-line tool you have installed. Their own privacy policies apply.
  • Email: if you add your Gmail address and app password, Aura reads and sends email only when you ask (for example "summarise my unread emails").
  • Web search and news: search words go to Tavily (if you add a key) and Google News; market prices come from Yahoo Finance.
  • Weather in the notch: your internet address is used to find your city (ipwho.is or ipapi.co), and the city's coordinates go to Open-Meteo for the forecast.
  • Notion: only if you add your own Notion token, and only the pages you share with it.
  • WhatsApp: messages are sent through the WhatsApp app on your PC, only to contacts you saved in Aura.

API keys, passwords and contacts you enter are saved only on your PC.

Children

Aura is not directed at children under 13.
