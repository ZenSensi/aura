Aura, your desktop assistant

A free, open-source Windows app. Type or say what you need and a team of AI agents does it: writes documents, opens apps, searches the web, reads your email, sends WhatsApp messages, plays music and puts reminders on your phone.

Download for Windows

What it does

  • Plans each request and hands the steps to specialist agents (Writer, Web, Inbox, Mailer, Files, Music, Tutor and more).
  • A dock or notch at the top of the screen, so you can ask Aura from any app.
  • Routines, reminders and workflows that run on a schedule.
  • Optional Google Tasks and Google Calendar connection, so reminders reach your phone and your upcoming events show in the notch.

How Aura uses Google

If you choose to connect Google, Aura asks for two permissions only: Google Tasks and Google Calendar events. It uses them to add the reminders you ask for and to show your upcoming events in the notch. The sign-in is saved only on your own PC, and nothing is sent to the developer.
